Help grow the baker’s own stake and baking power with a one-time donation. Suggested gifts:

  • 10 XTZ
  • 100 XTZ

Any amount is welcome. Donations are intended to help grow the baker’s own stake. They do not automatically stake funds, activate the baker or guarantee baking rights; operator action and protocol requirements still apply.

A donation is not delegation or direct staking. You transfer ownership of the donated XTZ to Sebuh.net, with no entitlement to repayment or rewards. Only give what you are comfortable giving permanently.

The small print, plainly

A few good questions.

Will I receive delegation rewards?

No. Sebuh.net does not distribute ordinary delegation rewards. Retained baking income supports Sebuh’s Tezos development. If receiving delegation payouts is your goal, choose a baker with a payout policy that fits you.

Do you take control of my wallet?

No. Ordinary delegation does not transfer ownership or give Sebuh.net permission to spend your funds. Never share a recovery phrase or private key. Connecting is optional; delegation requests are reviewed and approved in your wallet, not signed by our server.

What if I stake instead of just delegating?

Staking is a separate action. Protocol-distributed staking rewards still go to stakers under the baker’s active staking parameters, including its commission. Staked funds are subject to slashing and withdrawal delays. Review the current on-chain parameters before staking.

Can I stop supporting the baker?

You control your wallet and can change your delegation. If you also staked funds, changing delegates and withdrawing stake involve additional protocol rules and delays.

Is Sebuh.net baking right now?

The baker is being rebuilt. Activation, funding, eligibility and signing cutover are separate steps. This preview deliberately makes no live uptime, yield or reward claims.
